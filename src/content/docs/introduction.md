# Introduction: The Sovereign Shell

TerminaI is not just an "AI Terminal". It is a sovereign shell for governed
autonomy.

## What does that mean?

1.  **Sovereign**: It runs on _your_ machine, with _your_ tokens, respecting
    _your_ policies. It does not rely on a central SaaS for logic.
2.  **Governed**: Every action has a trust boundary. "Read Only" is fast.
    "Write" is checked. "Critical" requires explicit PIN approval.
3.  **Shell**: It is a true PTY bridge. It knows about your resizing, your
    colors, your history. It is an extension of your operating system.

## The 4-Fold Value Prop

TerminaI brings four capabilities to your environment:

- **Laptop Assistant**: Like "Android Assistant", but for your computer's OS.
- **Agentic Coordination**: It speaks A2A and MCP to coordinate other tools.
- **Endpoint Management**: It scales from your laptop to entire fleets.
- **Server Ops**: It provides a safe, audited way to repair servers.
