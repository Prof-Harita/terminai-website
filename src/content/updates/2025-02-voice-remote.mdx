# Voice and remote sessions

TerminaI is more than a local terminal. This update emphasizes voice-first control and
remote viewing across the site, with clearer guidance on sovereignty boundaries.

## What shipped
- Voice-first messaging on core pages.
- A remote viewing explanation tied to the trust ladder.
- Clear callouts on what stays local vs what can be relayed.

## Why it matters
- Users understand the difference between chatbot help and system operation.
- Enterprise teams get a clearer safety story from day one.
